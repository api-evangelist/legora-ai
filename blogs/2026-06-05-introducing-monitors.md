Legora has launched Monitors, a regulatory horizon scanning tool integrated into its aOS platform that automatically scans thousands of official sources across 100+ jurisdictions and alerts users to regulatory changes. Rather than relying on manual website checks and spreadsheets, the system continuously monitors official sources and delivers curated updates that teams can immediately act upon within the platform. The solution targets law firms seeking to enhance client advisory services and in-house legal teams needing efficient compliance tracking across multiple markets.
