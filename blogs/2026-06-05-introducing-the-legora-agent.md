Legora has released its Agent, an AI tool that automates complex legal work by planning, executing, and delivering results with minimal human oversight. The Agent can handle tasks like due diligence reviews, litigation matrices, and regulatory compliance analysis that previously required weeks of associate labor, dramatically reducing time-intensive work while preserving decisions requiring human judgment.
