Legora's agentic AI platform enables M&A transactions to close in under two weeks rather than months by automating document analysis, contract review, and synthesis work. The finance team can now lead deals independently using AI-powered tools like Tabular Review and Workflows, shifting from waiting on legal counsel to collaborating as partners. Each completed transaction refines processes for faster subsequent deals.
