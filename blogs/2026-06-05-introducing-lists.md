Legora has launched Lists, a new feature within its aOS platform designed to help legal teams organize, assign, and track work items like closing checklists and chronologies. The Legora Agent automatically populates list items from source documents with structured data, status updates, and assignees, while maintaining citations back to original sources. This tool aims to reduce manual work tracking and keep legal teams synchronized on shared matters.
