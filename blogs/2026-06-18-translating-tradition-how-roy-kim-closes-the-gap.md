Roy Kim built his practice in the space between two legal traditions. His firm Baystone Legal advises Korean businesses making inbound investments into Australia and local clients who prefer to communicate in Korean. Because Australia is a common law jurisdiction while Korea is not, Kim notes that literal translations do not work, so the work demands mapping legal concepts across entirely different frameworks rather than just language proficiency.
